Three ways we warehouse

Pricing below covers standard fulfillment. Heavier and bulk shipments run on separate, case-by-case terms.

STANDARD

Per-SKU Fulfillment

Packages under 2lb. $1 in / $1 out per SKU — receiving, storage, pick & pack, and shipping at the flat rate below.

SPECIALIZED

Heavy & High-Value Items

5–30kg packages, rugs and carpets, and jewelry up to $2,500/piece. Priced case-by-case rather than the flat SKU rate.

BULK

Full Container Load (FCL)

Bulk storage for pallet-packed, clearly labeled inventory arriving by the container, with prior notice. Forklift access available at added cost.
